# VerifiableFly — Onward Ticket Reservations & Flight Itineraries

**VerifiableFly** provides temporary flight reservations for travelers who need proof of onward travel, dummy tickets for visa applications, or flight itineraries for travel documentation.

🌐 **Website:** https://verifiablefly.com/

## About VerifiableFly

Preparing for an international trip can involve more than booking a flight. Airlines, embassies, visa application centers, and immigration authorities may request a flight itinerary or evidence of onward travel before departure or entry.

VerifiableFly offers reservation services designed to help travelers prepare the relevant documentation for their journeys.

Our reservations include a booking reference (PNR) that customers can use to check their booking details through the relevant airline's official website, where supported.

## Our Services

### 1. Onward Ticket Reservation

An onward ticket reservation shows a planned flight departing from the destination country. Travelers may need this documentation when preparing for airline check-in, immigration checks, or visa-free travel.

Learn more: [Onward Ticket & Proof of Onward Travel](https://verifiablefly.com/onward-ticket)

### 2. Dummy Ticket for Visa Applications

A dummy ticket is a temporary flight reservation that can be used as supporting travel documentation when a visa application requests a flight itinerary or reservation.

VerifiableFly provides reservation details, including passenger information, itinerary details, and a booking reference.

Learn more: [Dummy Ticket for Visa & Travel](https://verifiablefly.com/dummy-ticket)

### 3. Flight Reservation for Visa

Some visa applications require a proposed flight itinerary as part of the supporting documentation. A flight reservation can help applicants present their intended route and travel dates without purchasing a full-fare ticket in advance.

Learn more: [Flight Reservation for Visa Applications](https://verifiablefly.com/flight-reservation-for-visa)

## How It Works

1. **Provide your travel details:** Submit your passenger name, intended route, and travel dates.
2. **Reservation processing:** Our team processes the requested itinerary through its reservation workflow.
3. **Receive your documentation:** Receive your reservation details by email once processing is complete.
4. **Check your booking:** Use the booking reference and passenger surname on the relevant airline website to verify the reservation, where supported.

## Important Information

VerifiableFly provides temporary flight reservations, not fully paid flight tickets. A reservation is not a boarding pass and cannot be used to board an aircraft.

A valid booking reference does not guarantee acceptance by every airline, embassy, visa application center, or immigration authority. Requirements vary, so travelers should confirm the applicable rules with the organization reviewing their documents.

Always ensure that the itinerary and supporting documentation accurately reflect your travel plans and meet the relevant requirements.
